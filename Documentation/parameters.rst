the stable identifier used in preset files; the *id* is the position in the
host's parameter list.  Ids and symbols never change once released: new
parameters are only ever appended.  Parameters with id >= 62 are read-only
meters for the editor; they are hidden from hosts.

Hover help
==========

The text shown by the editor's TIPS tooltips, per parameter:

``master_on``
    Master power. Off bypasses the whole chain.
``intensity``
    How much of the whole chain you hear (blend with your untouched voice).
``in_gain``
    Input level into the chain.
``out_gain``
    Output level after the limiter-protected chain.
``out_sat``
    Warm saturation for a thicker, upfront voice.
``gate_thresh``
    Removes fan, AC and keyboard noise between words. Fully down = Off.
``dyn_on``
    Dynamics power (compressor + de-esser).
``dyn_style``
    Character of the compressor: Clean, Punch or Thick.
``comp_thresh``
    Level above which the compressor starts turning the voice down.
``comp_makeup``
    Brings the level back up after compression.
``comp_tone``
    Warmer (negative) or brighter (positive).
``comp_mix``
    Blend of compressed and dry voice (parallel compression).
``comp_mode``
    Reaction speed: Fast for rap, Slow for singing, Auto adapts to the
    voice.
``deess_freq``
    Centre frequency of the harsh 'sss' band.
``deess_amount``
    How strongly sibilance is tamed.
``deess_range``
    Maximum reduction applied to the 'sss' band.
``deess_listen``
    Hear exactly what the de-esser removes. Use it to find the right
    frequency.
``space_on``
    Space power (reverb, delay, chorus).
``space_type``
    Room, Hall or Plate reverb.
``reverb_mix``
    Room or hall sound around your voice.
``delay_mix``
    Tempo-synced echo level.
``reverb_duck``
    How far the reverb dips while you talk, so words stay clear.
``delay_duck``
    How far the echo dips while you talk, so words stay clear.
``delay_time``
    Echo time as a note length, synced to the host tempo (120 BPM if none).
